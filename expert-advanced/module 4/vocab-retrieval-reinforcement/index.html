Welcome

You met these twelve words and expressions during your Module 4 reading vocabulary hunt. Meeting a word once isn't enough for it to stick — this worksheet is built to bring each one back to memory several times, in different ways, so it moves from "I recognise this" to "I can actually use this."

  1. Type your name in the box at the top right — it stays there as you move through every page, so do it now before you forget.
  2. Move between pages with the Back / Next buttons at the bottom of each page, or jump straight to any page by clicking its numbered dot at the top.
  3. Page 2 is a reference bank of all twelve items. Visit it whenever you need a reminder — there's no expectation that you memorise it before starting the exercises.
  4. On the checked exercises (matching, gap-fill, multiple choice, and the sorting task) your answers are marked automatically as you work and re-checked when you submit.
  5. The writing task near the end is not auto-marked — I'll read what you write myself and give you personal feedback on it.
  6. On the final page, press Check & Submit. You'll see your score immediately, and I'll receive a copy — including a note of exactly which items to go back over — so we can talk about your trickiest ones together.
