Stage 5 / 5 — Freer Practice

Write the Case Report

Think of a real (or invented) situation where something went a bit wrong — a mix-up, a lost item, an awkward decision, a misunderstanding. Write 6–8 sentences telling the story and reflecting on it. Try to naturally include:

  • at least one deduction about what must/can't have happened
  • at least one piece of advice or self-criticism (should have / shouldn't have)
  • a mix of at least 6 different modal forms overall
