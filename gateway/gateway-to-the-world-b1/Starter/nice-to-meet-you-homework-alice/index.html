Do, Are or Have? Look at the main verb!

Do you + action verb: Do you like sport? Do you play the piano?

Are you + noun / adjective / -ing: Are you a sports fan? Are you happy? Are you listening?

Have you got …?: Have you got any brothers or sisters?

Are you like sport? → Do you like sport?

Numbers: -teen or -ty?

thirteen (13) — the stress is at the end: thirTEEN · thirty (30) — the stress is at the start: THIRty

12 = twelve · 20 = twenty · 34 = thirty-four (with a hyphen)

Present simple or present continuous?

Present simple = habits and facts: I go to music school every day. (every day, usually, sometimes, at the weekend)

Present continuous = now or around now: I’m reading a book about Egypt at the moment. (now, right now, at the moment, this week)

State verbs (love, like, know, have) — no -ing: I’m loving Chopin. → I love Chopin.
