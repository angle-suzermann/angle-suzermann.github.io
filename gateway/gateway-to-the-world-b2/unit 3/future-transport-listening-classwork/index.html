Personalised discussion

Talk about it

Discuss these questions with a partner. This part is not scored — just talk, and use the notes box for any ideas you want to remember.

1. Think of an idea that people said "it'll never happen" — but it did. What was it, and how did it become reality?

2. Musk's number one obsession is Mars. What's your own number one obsession right now — in study, work, or life?

3. Describe a project you're doing a lot of work on at the moment. What steps have you already taken towards finishing it? Is there a lot to do yet?

4. Which of the future transport ideas from the podcast (electric planes, reusable rockets, the Hyperloop) do you think will actually happen in your lifetime? Which is just science fiction?
