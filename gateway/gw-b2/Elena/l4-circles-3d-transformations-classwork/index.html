Maths in English · Lesson 4 lexical version

Circle geometry, regular polygons, solids, lines and planes in space, and transformations. This is the biggest gap between the Russian and the English names, so the focus is on the words.

By the end of this lesson you can:
  • name the parts of a circle and the circle theorems in English (chord, arc, tangent, the angle at the centre);
  • find arc lengths, sector areas, volumes and surface areas in English;
  • name solids, their parts and cross-sections, and describe lines in space;
  • describe a transformation fully (a rotation of 90° about the origin).
Remember: длина окружности is the circumference, вписанный / описанный is inscribed in / circumscribed about, правильный многоугольник is a regular polygon, and гомотетия is an enlargement.

1. Read the question

Choose an answer. You see the explanation straight away.

1. длина окружности in English is…

длина окружности = the circumference. Длина дуги = arc length.

2. “The chord AB subtends an angle of 80° at the centre O.” This means…

The angle at the centre made by the ends of the chord: angle AOB = 80°.

3. ABCD is a cyclic quadrilateral. This means…

Cyclic = вписанный: its vertices lie on one circle.

4. “Give the exact area of the sector.” You write…

Exact = keep π. 25.1 is a rounded value.

5. гомотетия in English is…

IB says enlargement (with a scale factor and a centre).

6. “Describe fully the single transformation” means you must give…

For example: a rotation of 90° anticlockwise about the origin. Missing details lose marks.

Operation · notice the structure

A. How a solution is built

Problem 1. A sector has radius 8 cm and angle 45°. Find its exact area.

These are the lines of a model solution, mixed up. Click them in the right order.

The sector is 45/360 = 1/8 of the circle.
The area of the whole circle is π × 8² = 64π cm².
So the area of the sector is 64π ÷ 8.
The area of the sector is 8π cm².
