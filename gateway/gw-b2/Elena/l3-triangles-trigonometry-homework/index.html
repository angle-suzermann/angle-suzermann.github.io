2. What does the question want?

1. cos θ in a right-angled triangle is…

SOH CAH TOA: cos = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse.

2. You know two sides and the angle between them. You want the third side. Use…

Two sides + the included angle → the cosine rule.

3. You know two angles and one side. You want another side. Use…

Pairs of “side and opposite angle” → the sine rule.

4. Area = ½ab sin C needs…

C must be the included angle: the angle between sides a and b.

5. All the lengths of a shape are multiplied by 3. The area is multiplied by…

Areas scale by the square of the scale factor: 3² = 9.

6. The angle of depression is measured…

Elevation: looking up. Depression: looking down. Both from the horizontal.
