Grammar reference

Complete the rules and examples

Fill each gap with one word (or a short phrase). Check your answers against the Grammar reference on page 106 of the Student's Book afterwards.

Use

We use the passive when:

■ we are more interested in the than the people who do it.

■ we do not exactly who does the action.

■ it is or understood who does the action.

Form

subject + be + (+ by + agent)

Examples

Present simple: Children taught to run to the centre of the playground.

Present perfect: It has shown that quick reactions make all the difference.

Present continuous: The volcano observed for further activity today.

Past simple: An experiment conducted.

will: All tomorrow's flights cancelled if the ash cloud is blown over the flight path.

can: In droughts, forest fires started very easily.
