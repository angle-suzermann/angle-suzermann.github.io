This homework practises passive report structures — the grammar we use to talk about what "people" say, think, believe, or claim, without naming exactly who. The photo behind this page is Earth seen from the International Space Station — a good reminder that a lot of what we now know about space, and about our own planet, used to be just a rumour or a guess. Grammar reminder

1. It + is/was + reporting verb + that + clause
It is said that the universe is expanding. · It was thought that the Earth was flat.
2. Subject + is/was + reporting verb + to + infinitive (same time as the report)
She is believed to be the best astronomer on the team.
3. Subject + is/was + reporting verb + to have + past participle (earlier than the report — perfect infinitive)
He is believed to have discovered the comet in 1997.

Common reporting verbs:

saythinkbelieveknowclaimreportunderstandexpectconsiderallege

Exercise 2 6 items — Changing Beliefs

Each pair of sentences contrasts an old belief with what we know now. Rewrite the bold verbs as passive report structures. Sentence 1 is done for you.
Example

People used to think the Earth was flat. Now we know it is round.

→ It was thought that the Earth was flat. Now it is known that the Earth is round.

2. People used to think everything was made of fire, earth, air and water. Now we know there are over a hundred elements.

It that everything was made of fire, earth, air and water. Now it that there are over a hundred elements.

3. People used to think the Moon was a smooth ball. Now we know it has valleys and mountains.

It that the Moon was a smooth ball. Now it that it has valleys and mountains.

4. People used to say feelings came from your liver. Now people say they come from your heart.

It that feelings came from your liver. Now it that they come from your heart.

5. People used to think having a bath once a month was enough. Now we believe we need a bath or shower every day.

It that having a bath once a month was enough. Now it that we need a bath or shower every day.

6. People used to believe kings were gods. Now we know they are only human.

It that kings were gods. Now it that they are only human.

7. People used to think our bodies constantly made new blood. Now we know the body circulates blood.

It that our bodies constantly made new blood. Now it that the body circulates blood.

Exercise 3 4 items — Same Meaning

Complete the two sentences so they mean the same as the sentence given, using the word in capitals. Do not change the word given. Use between two and five words in each gap.
Example

The newspaper report said no one had been killed in the tsunami. (REPORTED)

It was reported that no one had been killed in the tsunami.

The tsunami was reported to have killed no one.

2. People always say that this town is the most polluted in the country. (SAID)

It this town is the most polluted in the country.

This town the most polluted in the country.

3. People know dinosaurs lived on Earth millions of years ago. (KNOWN)

It that dinosaurs lived on Earth millions of years ago.

Dinosaurs on Earth millions of years ago.

4. Everyone believes the team needs a new manager. (BELIEVED)

It the team needs a new manager.

The team a new manager.

5. People have claimed the landslide was the worst this century. (CLAIMED)

It the landslide was the worst this century.

The landslide the worst this century.

Where to review

Quick reference — reporting verbs

say / said — general report
think / thought — opinion, often outdated
believe / believed — widely held opinion
know / known — accepted as fact
claim / claimed — stated, not yet proven
report / reported — official / news source
understand / understood — informal report
expect / expected — prediction
