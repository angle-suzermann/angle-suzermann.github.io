Sam

"Er, to decide what charities to give my money to, erm, I normally like to speak to someone who works for the charity, 'cos I think you can get the best impression of what they're about by actually speaking to a person involved. "I have raised money for a charity. I once spent a day raising money for an environmental charity at my school. Er, we sold cakes and held events, such as raffles, to make money. And it was a good, fun day."

"I think the best way to raise money for charities is human interaction. So, face-to-face, because people react much better to being able to talk to another human being rather than just to an advert on television which you can just turn off or ignore. Um, and it's much easier to explore issues and get a sense of the human element of charity if you're actually talking to another person."

"I think harassing people on the street should be avoided as a form of charity fundraising, just because it doesn't really make sense to bully people into donating money to a positive organisation. Um, but on the other hand, I do think that interacting with people is very important in raising awareness of charity."

Vanessa

"Um, for me to decide upon a charity I think it's one that tends to affect the people that I know, around me, so that I feel that I can make a difference and help the people that I like and love."

"Er, I have done a fundraising event for a charity, um, for a health charity, in which children from the local village — and many helpers — did a bake sale and put on a performance for the people in the village, um, to raise money to send to that charity."

"For me, personally, I think the best way to raise money for charities is to put on events of local towns and cities. Um, this way people are more prepared to spend more money for the charity, because they don't happen very often."

"Um, for me personally I don't think there are any bad ways to raise money for charities. However, we have to make sure that all the proceeds that are raised do eventually go to the charity."

Bea

"I think I prefer to give money to charities that I'm personally involved in or have friends or family that are part of, because then I kind of have a connection to that charity."

"I've actually done a lot of fundraising for different charities. Um, so I did some with cancer charities, and with disaster charities. "Personally, from my experience of raising money for charity, I think that the best way is when you can actually get people involved in the charity. Skills 4 Life — research a charity

Not scored — this is a short research project. Tick each step off as you complete it.
  • Choose one of the charities you wrote down at the start, or think of another one.
  • Go to the charity's website and read about its history, its work, how it raises funds, and who makes donations. Make notes as you go.
  • Turn your notes into a short fact file you could show your classmates.
