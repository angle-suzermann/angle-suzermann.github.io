Speaking practice · ungraded

🗣️ Practise talking about the statistics

Workbook p.71, exercise 6

Use the pie charts, the graph, and the corrected information from Exercise 5 to talk about the statistics out loud (to yourself, a classmate, or record yourself). Use these cues to help structure what you say:

  • Start with the overall trend: "The graph shows that..."
  • Give a number or proportion: "...tripled / a quarter / one in three..."
  • Compare the two pie charts (2012 vs 2013): "If we compare the two years..."
  • Use at least three trend words from the Vocabulary boost page (e.g. fluctuated, steadily, the majority).
  • Finish with your own opinion: "I think the biggest problem is... because..."
Study skills · ungraded

💡 Study skills

How can you cope if you can't remember a word while speaking?

Try: describing what the word means in other words (paraphrasing) · using a more general word instead (e.g. "a kind of natural disaster") · using gesture or drawing · saying "it's like..." and giving an example · asking your partner/teacher "How do you say...?"
