Warm-up: Do you need a hand?

Think about helping people before we meet Becky, Rachel, Tom and Mark.

1. Do you think you're a helpful person? Why / why not?

2. Do you know anyone who's very helpful? Have they helped you? What did they do?

3. "Help!" brainstorm — what kinds of help do people give and receive? Write at least 4 ideas.

Example categories: moving/lifting things, jobs around the house, help with studies, technical help, emotional support, advice
