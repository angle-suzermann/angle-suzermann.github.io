Three people, three websites

Show script / Показать текст

🗣️ Sheena "Last year, I had some free time and a bit of money to spend. I'd always wanted to go walking and climbing in the Himalayas, but I didn't want to go on my own and none of my friends wanted to go. So I found a website called Travel Groups, where you can contact other people who want to go to the same places as you, and you can join up and go together as a group. Anyway, I found three other people to go with and we all met in Delhi in North India and we travelled together. It worked out really well. I'm quite self-confident and I think I'm an extrovert, so I think I'm quite good at getting on with people and making friends."

🗣️ Alya "I had a few months off after university, so I looked on the internet for volunteer work and found a really good website for last-minute volunteer jobs called the Volunteer Community Project. It was good because you can arrange things straight away and they pay your fares, and you get basic accommodation and food. You don't earn money, but you don't really spend much either. I went to London and worked there with young children from problem families. I didn't have any experience, but that doesn't matter. Actually it seems like I've got a natural talent for teaching children."

🗣️ Brad "I really needed to earn some money and I couldn't get a proper job. Someone told me about this website called Short Work, where people offer short-term jobs for a few days or a couple of weeks. I'm quite good at things like that, and I know a bit about electricity and plumbing. I found this advert for a family who've got a large house and needed someone to do some basic work on it. I think they could see that I was quite serious about it, and I was determined to work hard."

Match each website with its purpose.

Reduced expressions

Adverts often use "reduced" expressions — short phrases that leave out words like is/are and a/the to sound direct and attention-grabbing.

Find the reduced expression in the adverts that means the same as each sentence below.

1. You don’t need any experience. →

2. We’d prefer a person with climbing experience. →

3. We need a volunteer. →

4. We don’t require you to have any qualifications. →

Now do the opposite: expand each reduced expression into a full sentence. (These are checked by your teacher, not automatically — there's more than one good answer.)
