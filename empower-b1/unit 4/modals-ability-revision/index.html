Warm-up · trust your instinct

Before we explain anything...

A revision workshop for can, could, be able to and manage to — built to reinforce Unit 4A, not repeat it. Every task below uses new sentences and new situations.

Try these four sentences first. Don't worry about the rule yet — just pick what sounds right. We'll check the logic together on the next page. (This page isn't scored — it's just to get you thinking.)

1When I was seven, I ___ ride a bike without stabilisers.

2It was really difficult, but in the end she ___ finish the marathon.

3Next year, I ___ speak Japanese fluently, I hope!

4I ___ speak French since I was a child.

Notice anything? Four different situations needed four different forms. That's the whole topic — let's map it out.
