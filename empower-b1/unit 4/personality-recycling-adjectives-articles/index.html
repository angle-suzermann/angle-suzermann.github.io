Articles — the rules, refreshed

Grammar Focus 4B, SB p.150 — quick reference before you start

• a/an = one of several, or a new topic: I read a great book last week.
• the = the only one, or already known: Where are the car keys?
• the before superlatives: Kyoto is the most beautiful city in Japan.
• no article for people/things in general: Cats are clever animals.
• no article in meals and fixed phrases: have breakfast, go home, go to bed, go to school
• no article with most countries/cities, but the with the USA, the Amazon, the Bahamas, etc.
