know very little aboutknow very little about + noun

know almost nothing about something

“Honestly, I know very little about lynxes.” from the dialogue

Watch out: little, not few (we use few with countable plurals), and the preposition is about: not I know few about….

be responsible forbe responsible + for + noun / -ing

it is your job to take care of something

“The centre is responsible for about twenty of them.” from the dialogue

Watch out: the preposition is for: not responsible of, not responsible to the animals.

be endangeredbe endangered · an endangered species / animal

in danger of disappearing for ever

“In some parts of Europe they're endangered, because people have destroyed the forests where they live.” from the dialogue

Watch out: endangered is an adjective: they're endangered. With a noun we say in danger: the lynx is in danger, not in endangered.

protect animals / natureprotect + animals / the environment / the forests

keep something safe from damage

“They protect lynxes there.” from the dialogue

Watch out: no preposition before the thing you protect: protect lynxes. For the danger we use from: protect lynxes from hunters.

take part intake part + in + a project / a clean-up

be one of the people doing an activity

“Have you ever taken part in a project like this before?” from the dialogue

Watch out: take part in: not take part to, not take a part. (Participate in means the same, but it is more formal.)

a conservation projecta (turtle / wildlife) conservation project · group · centre

work to protect nature and animals

“I helped with a turtle conservation project in Greece.” from the dialogue

Watch out: noun + noun: a turtle conservation project. Not a project of conservation of turtles, and not conservative (a false friend).

volunteer atvolunteer + at (a place) / for (an organisation)

work without pay because you want to help

“I'm going to volunteer at a wildlife centre for two weeks.” from the dialogue

Watch out: volunteer is also a verb: I'm going to volunteer, not I'm going to be volunteer. Place = at, organisation = for.

breathe cleaner airbreathe + cleaner / fresh / polluted + air

take air into your body

“You'll breathe cleaner air for two weeks!” from the dialogue

Watch out: cleaner air (short adjective + -er), not more clean air. We breathe air; in everyday English we don't say inhale.

help to feedhelp (to) + verb · help someone (to) + verb

do something useful together with others

“I'll probably help to feed the animals and clean their enclosures.” from the dialogue

Watch out: help to feed or help feed: not help feeding, not help for feed.

it's essential toit's essential + to + verb

very important: you must do it

“The villagers think it's essential to keep the forest quiet at night.” example

Watch out: essential to + verb (it's essential to wash your hands); with a noun: essential for the project. Not essential for wash.

prevent air pollutionprevent + pollution / accidents / fires

stop something bad before it happens

“That also prevents air pollution, and the lynxes are safer.” example

Watch out: prevent + noun straight after it. With a person: prevent someone from + -ing. Not prevent to….

limit the number oflimit the number of + plural noun (cars, visitors)

keep something under a maximum

“They want to limit the number of cars on the mountain road.” example

Watch out: the number of + plural noun: limit the number of visitors, not limit the visitors number.

My green year

by Mila, 17

This year I'm going to make some changes. I've thought about it a lot, and I'm not going to buy any new clothes for six months. I'll swap them with my friends instead. I've also joined a conservation project in the national park near my town. We plant trees on the first Saturday of every month, and next weekend we're meeting a scientist who studies lynxes. I know very little about them, so I'm really excited!

Will it be easy? Probably not. My friends think I'll give up after a month. But I don't think I will. My mum has promised she'll help, and she says she'll walk to work when the weather is good.

And look at our park: the river is cleaner this year and the birds are back. It's going to be a great summer for wildlife!
