Reading: a biography

Read the biography, then answer the questions.

Never too late: the story of Rita Moss

Rita Moss was born in 1950 in a small fishing town in Cornwall. Her father was a fisherman, and she used to spend hours on the beach watching the surfers, but her parents didn't allow her to go in the water. 'Girls didn't do that kind of thing,' she remembers. 'My mother was very old-fashioned.'

Rita left school at sixteen and worked in a bakery for ten years. During that time she met Tom, a quiet, reserved man who took after his father: they were both fishermen and both very shy. They got married in 1972 and had three children.

For the next forty years, Rita was too busy to think about surfing. While Tom was at sea, she looked after the children and ran the family business. Meanwhile, the town was changing: tourists were arriving every summer and surf schools were opening along the beach.

In 2020, two years after Tom had died, Rita's granddaughter Mia persuaded her to try a lesson. 'I was terrified,' Rita laughs. 'I couldn't swim very well, and the water was absolutely freezing!' She fell off the board forty times that day. But she didn't give up. After three months of lessons, she managed to stand up on a wave for the first time.

Today, at 76, Rita surfs three times a week until the weather gets too cold. She has become quite famous: a video of her surfing has had over two million views. 'People ask me if I'm brave,' she says. 'I'm not. I'm just determined. It's never too late to do the thing you always wanted to do.'

Task
Write a short biography (120–150 words) of someone in your family, or someone you know, who has done something interesting.
  • When and where were they born? What did they use to do?
  • Tell the important events in order. Use narrative tenses (was working… had moved… met).
  • Use time expressions: for, during, while, meanwhile, until.
  • What can they do / what did they manage to do? What are they like?
Before you submit: check your feedback patterns
