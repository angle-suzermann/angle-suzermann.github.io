Wiseman Teens A2 · Lesson 14 November

Today we work on the mistakes from your homework: the lesson sentences, the email to Tom and the story. Then we speak about two pictures.

After this lesson you can:
  • correct your own typical mistakes without help: went, go, is + -ing, will be, hear from, on foot;
  • write an email of 35–50 words with plans (we’re going to… / we can go…) in 10 minutes;
  • keep a story inside 60 words;
  • talk about yesterday and tomorrow in the right tense.
Rule of the day: one verb form is enough. we are went → we went, I am go → I go, I am be → I will be.

3 · Dates in Listening

In the homework you heard 22nd May but wrote 27. Numbers like twenty-two / twenty-seven, thirteen / thirty sound close. Write the date the way you can write it in the exam.

You can write: 22nd May, 22 May or May 22nd. Ordinals: first, second, third, fourth … twenty-first, twenty-second, thirtieth.

Only answer the task: Tom didn’t write to you first, so don’t start with “In your email you asked me…” — it takes 10 words from your limit.

8 · Speaking · Picture 3 — Tomorrow

Look at the picture and answer aloud. Try to speak for 1 minute (⏱ timer on the left).
