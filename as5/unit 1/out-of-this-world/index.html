Out of This World: Past Simple & Past Continuous

A new world record!

On 14th October, 2012, Felix Baumgartner jumped from space using only a parachute. He travelled faster than the speed of sound. He flew 39 km into the sky in a special capsule and then he prepared to jump. He was checking his spacesuit when he discovered a problem with his helmet. However, he decided to continue. While he was standing on the edge of the capsule he said, ‘I’m coming home now.’ And then he jumped. But there was another problem. He was passing through the sound barrier when he started to spin. This was very dangerous. Luckily, he was able to stop spinning and landed safely.

When he jumped from the capsule, eight million people were watching on YouTube. One person said, ‘I closed my eyes while he was stepping off the capsule. It was terrifying.’ Another said, ‘I was watching music videos when I saw Felix jump. Incredible!’

Remember: past continuous (was/were + -ing) = the action already in progress; past simple = the action that interrupted it. He was checking his suit when he discovered a problem.

Exploring the Arctic

Yesterday the sun was shining. I was having breakfast on the boat when we saw a polar bear. I was watching it when it jumped into the water and disappeared. Later that morning I was taking photos of an iceberg when a large piece of ice fell off. It was an incredible sight. Later, while I was looking at the sea, a whale jumped right out of the water. In the evening, we were making dinner when someone pointed to the sky. It was red, green, blue and violet! While we were looking at the sky, someone explained that these were the Northern Lights.

Part 2 — Choose the correct form (interrupting action)

I was having breakfast on the boat when we ___ (see) a polar bear.

I was watching the polar bear when it ___ (jump) into the water.

I was taking photos of an iceberg when a large piece of ice ___ (fall) off.

While I was looking at the sea, a whale ___ (jump) right out of the water.

We were making dinner when someone ___ (point) to the sky.

Part 3 — Complete with the past continuous (background action)

I (have) breakfast on the boat when we saw a polar bear.

I (watch) the polar bear when it jumped into the water.

Later that morning I (take) photos of an iceberg when a large piece of ice fell off.

While I (look) at the sea, a whale jumped right out of the water.

While we (look) at the sky, someone explained that these were the Northern Lights.

Part 4 — Complete the story (combine two actions)

I (take) photos when an Arctic fox appeared.

I was taking photos of the fox when I (drop) my camera in the snow.

We (look) for my camera when the fox ran away.

I was cleaning my camera while the seals (jump) into a water hole.
