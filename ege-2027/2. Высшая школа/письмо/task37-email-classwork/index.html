Warm-up: useful phrases

Make sure you understand the meaning of the following words and phrases. Try to use them in your email on the next page.

As for my advice about
used to introduce your own opinion or advice on a topic someone asked you about
As for my advice about your career, I think you should follow your own interests.
If you ask me about… I can say...
a way to give a personal opinion when someone has directly asked for it
If you ask me about the best subject, I can say it's maths.
to apply for admission
to formally ask to be accepted onto a course or into a university/college
She's going to apply for admission to a medical university.
For example,
used to introduce a specific example that supports what you just said
Some jobs are very stressful. For example, being a doctor means long, irregular hours.
to follow one's decision
to support and go along with a choice someone has made, rather than opposing it
My parents always follow my decisions about my future.
(not) to provide support
to (not) give someone help, encouragement or backing
Unfortunately, my parents don't provide much support when it comes to choosing a career.
In your letter you told me about
a standard phrase for referring back to news your pen-friend mentioned
In your letter you told me about your new puppy — congratulations!
to take exams in
to sit/write official exams in a particular subject
I'm planning to take exams in maths and physics next year.
to get advice from smb
to ask someone for their opinion or guidance before making a decision
I always get advice from my parents before making a big decision.
to enter
(in this context) to be admitted to and start studying at a university
He hopes to enter a good university next year.

Task 37. Write an email

You have received an email message from your English-speaking pen-friend James.
From: James@mail.uk
To: Russian_friend@ege.ru
Subject: Plans for the future

… My parents want me to follow in their footsteps and become a doctor. But my dream is to study maths. What would you advise me to do? What are your plans after you leave school? Do your parents support you in your decision?

My parents bought me a puppy for my birthday! …
Write an email to James.
In your message:
  • answer his questions
  • ask 3 questions about the puppy
Write 100–140 words. Remember the rules of email writing.
