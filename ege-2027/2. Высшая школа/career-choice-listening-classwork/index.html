Choosing a Career — Listening Classwork

Today's classwork brings together three ЕГЭ listening tasks about growing up and making choices: six speakers share their views on public libraries, a school-leaver named Jake tells his teacher what career he's chosen, and a university student explains why he thinks the Student Union doesn't really represent students like him. You'll warm up with the vocabulary you'll meet in the recordings, then work through each listening task at your own pace.
