Артикли и предлоги

Артикль, предлог, название

Имя правильно звучит, только если рядом правильный артикль и предлог.

1.
We walked across ___ Red Square.

Red Square — без артикля.

2.
We visited ___ Hermitage.

the Hermitage, the Kremlin, the Bolshoi.

3.
We saw ___ Kremlin.

the Kremlin.

4.
We swam in ___ Lake Baikal.

Lake + название — без артикля.

5.
We sailed along ___ Volga.

Реки — с the.

6.
We took ___ Trans-Siberian Railway.

the Trans-Siberian Railway.

7.
We visited ___ St Basil’s Cathedral.

Перед притяжательным St Basil’s артикля нет.

8.
Gagarin was the first person to go ___ space.

Движение — go into space; быть там — be in space.

9.
Chekhov wrote The Cherry ___.

The Cherry Orchard — вишнёвый сад как насаждение.

10.
For a British friend, the Great Patriotic War is best explained as part of…

the Great War — это Первая мировая. Для иностранца: the Second World War (1939–1945), а Великая Отечественная — 1941–1945.

Every year thousands of tourists come to Russia to see its famous sights. In Moscow they walk across Red Square, visit the Kremlin and admire St Basil's Cathedral. Many of them go to a performance at the Bolshoi Theatre, because Russian ballet is famous all over the world. Then they take a train to St Petersburg, the city founded by Peter the Great. There they visit the Hermitage, which is located in the Winter Palace, the former home of the tsars. Some travellers choose a longer journey on the Trans-Siberian Railway to Lake Baikal. On the way they learn about Tolstoy, Tchaikovsky and Yuri Gagarin, who became the first person to go into space in 1961.
