Прочитай и заметь блоки

Прочитай тексты целиком: жёлтым выделены чанки. Потом найди в текстах блок для каждой русской фразы.

Background · 1861 · The end of serfdom

In 1861 Tsar Alexander II abolished serfdom, freeing millions of peasants. For this reason he is often called the Tsar Liberator. His reforms also changed the courts, the army and local government.

1917 · Two revolutions

In 1917 Russia went through two revolutions. In February the monarchy fell, and in October the Bolsheviks, led by Vladimir Lenin, seized power. After a long civil war, the Soviet Union was formed in 1922.

1941–1945 · The Great Patriotic War

On 22 June 1941 Nazi Germany attacked the Soviet Union. In Russia this war is called the Great Patriotic War; it was part of the Second World War. It ended in victory in May 1945, but the country lost about 27 million people.

1945–1991 · The Cold War and the Space Race

After the war, the USSR and the USA were rivals in the Cold War, a long period of political tension without direct fighting between the two countries. One part of it was the Space Race: the Soviet Union launched the first satellite in 1957 and sent the first man into space in 1961.

1985–1991 · Perestroika and a new Russia

In 1985 Mikhail Gorbachev came to power and introduced perestroika (restructuring) and glasnost (openness). In December 1991 the Soviet Union ceased to exist, and Russia became an independent state. Boris Yeltsin, elected in June 1991, became the first President of Russia.

          Nurmagomed Gadzhimagomedov

          Nurmagomed Gadzhimagomedov was born in 1996 in Dagestan. He became an officer of the Airborne Forces, served in Syria and later commanded a company. He was killed on 24 February 2022, the first day of the military operation in Ukraine. According to official reports, he was wounded and surrounded, and he detonated a grenade. By a decree of 3 March 2022 he was posthumously awarded the title Hero of Russia; he was the first serviceman to receive it for actions in this conflict.

          Archpriest Mikhail Vasiliev

          Archpriest Mikhail Vasiliev (1971–2022) graduated from Moscow State University and the Academy of the General Staff and was ordained a priest in 1998. As a military chaplain, he travelled to Kosovo, Bosnia, Syria and other conflict zones to support soldiers. He served as rector of the church at the headquarters of the Strategic Missile Forces. He was killed on 6 November 2022 in the zone of the military operation while performing his pastoral duties and was posthumously awarded the title Hero of Russia.
