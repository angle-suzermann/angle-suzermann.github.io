Как пользоваться тренажёром

Задание 38: комментарий к данным + своё мнение

Тренажёр подходит и для урока, и для самостоятельной подготовки дома. Он построен на двух реальных кейсах ЕГЭ («Путешествия за рубеж» и «Путешествия по России»). Формулировки заданий, тексты и данные — дословно из экзаменационных материалов.

Что внутри

— Чек-лист и план-схема — опоры, к которым можно возвращаться на любой странице.
— Два кейса, в каждом: задание на перефраз, задание на лексику и итоговое письменное высказывание (Exam Task, 200–250 слов).
— Упражнения на лексику и сопоставление проверяются автоматически — счётчик наверху показывает результат.
— Перефраз и эссе — творческая работа, автопроверки для них нет.

Exam Task

Imagine that you are doing a project on why people in Zetland travel abroad. You have found some data on the subject – the results of the opinion polls (see the table below). Comment on the data in the table and give your opinion on the subject of the project.

ReasonNumber of respondents (%)
to see famous sights40
to get out of comfort zone22
to practise a foreign language17
to meet new people12
to taste local food9

Write 200–250 words.

Use the following plan:

– make an opening statement on the subject of the project;
– select and report 2-3 main facts;
– make 1-2 comparisons where relevant and give your comments;
– outline a problem that can arise with travelling abroad and suggest a way of solving it;
– conclude by giving and explaining your opinion on the importance of travelling in human life.

Tip! The following introductory phrases and linking words can help you structure your answer and make it logical and coherent.

  • It is a well-known fact that
  • The project aims to outline
  • Analyzing the data from the table
  • Another important feature
  • What is more
  • In comparison with
  • As for a problem that can arise with
  • In conclusion, I would like to say that I think

Образец (273 слова, из авторского кейса):

It is a well-known fact that travelling is becoming more and more popular nowadays and a lot of people decide to spend holidays somewhere abroad. I have found some data for my project on why people from Zetland travel overseas and I would like to make comments on it.

The data table provides statistics on the reasons. What stands out in the table is that nearly half of the respondents (40%) marked sightseeing as the main reason for going overseas.

By looking at the data table, it can also be seen that practising a foreign language is surprisingly not among the most popular goals for travelling abroad. It makes up only 17% of the people who took part in the survey.

I would also like to make a comparison. The data from the table demonstrates that seeing famous sights is a more than four times more popular reason for travelling abroad than tasting local food, with 40 and 9 per cent of the respondents respectively. It seems quite natural because for many people going overseas is usually associated with visiting places of interest and seeing well-known landmarks rather than focusing on eating.

It is worth mentioning that certain problems can arise with travelling abroad. One of them is the language barrier. If one does not know a foreign language, they will face communication difficulties.

One of the possible solutions is signing up for language courses to brush up on their language skills.

In conclusion, I would like to express my opinion and say that travelling provides fantastic opportunities to discover something new. People get acquainted with the culture and traditions of the places they visit.
