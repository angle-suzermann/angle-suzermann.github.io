Why Travelling Is Important

Today you will work with an authentic video about the benefits of travelling. First you will watch it and answer True / False / Not Stated questions, then restore the text. After that you will find twelve useful phrases in the video script, learn how they work, and use them in a few sentences of your own. At the end you send your work to your teacher.

Patterns · Page 7 of 10

Operation · meaning and pattern

Task 6. The phrases and their patterns

By the end of this page you can say which preposition or verb each phrase needs.

Tap a card to open it. Look at the pattern, then read the Watch out note: it shows the most common mistake, often a translation from Russian.

get away fromget away from + your everyday life / work / the city

leave a place or routine for a while to rest

“Travelling is a great opportunity to temporarily get away from your everyday life” from the video

Watch out: the preposition is from: get away from, not get away of.

from a different point of viewlook at something + from a different point of view / from the outside

in a new way, as another person would see it

“look at it from a different point of view” from the video

Watch out: the preposition is from, not in / on. From my point of view = in my opinion.

deal with issuesdeal with + issues / problems / stress

give your attention to a problem and try to sort it out

“It’s so much easier to deal with issues and solve problems” from the video

Watch out: always with with: deal with a problem.

solve problemssolve + a problem / problems

find an answer to a problem

“It’s so much easier to deal with issues and solve problems” from the video

Watch out: «решать проблему» = solve a problem, not decide a problem (decide = make a choice).

make decisionsmake + a decision / better / healthy decisions

decide what to do

“make healthy decisions” from the video

Watch out: «принять решение» = make a decision, not accept a decision.

live in / be out of your comfort zonelive in + your comfort zone · get / be out of + your comfort zone

stay where everything is safe and familiar · leave that safe place

“You’re out of your comfort zone” from the video

Watch out: get out of your comfort zone, not go out from comfort zone; use your / my / our.

be exposed tobe exposed to + different cultures / new ideas

have the chance to see and experience something

“You’re exposed to different cultures” from the video

Watch out: to is a preposition here: exposed to new ideas, not exposed with.

face challengesface + a challenge / challenges / problems

have to deal with something difficult

“When you travel, you get into unusual situations and face different challenges” from the video

Watch out: face a challenge (no with); be faced with a challenge is also correct.

push your limitspush + your / my limits

try to do more than you thought you could

“It makes you push your limits” from the video

Watch out: we say push your limits, not push your borders.

handle thingshandle + a situation / stress / things (no preposition)

deal with something successfully

“handle things better” from the video

Watch out: no preposition: handle stress, not handle with stress.

come up with creative solutionscome up with + an idea / a solution

think of a new, clever way to solve a problem

“and come up with creative solutions” from the video

Watch out: with, not to: come up to someone = walk towards them.

spend money onspend money / time + on + noun / -ing

use money to pay for something

“It is better to spend your money on experiences rather than on material things” from the video

Watch out: the preposition is on: spend money on clothes, not for.

Possible or not? · Page 8 of 10

Operation · judge: possible or not?

Task 9. Possible or not natural?

Is the phrase used correctly? Choose, then read why. Most of the “not natural” sentences are word-for-word translations from Russian.

1. Travelling helps you decide your problems.

«решать проблему» = solve a problem: Travelling helps you solve your problems.

2. I accepted a decision to study in Moscow.

«принять решение» = make a decision: I made a decision to study in Moscow.

3. Sometimes we have to face with a challenge.

face a challenge (no with), or be faced with a challenge.

4. Travelling teaches you to handle stress.

Correct: handle + noun, no preposition.

5. I spend a lot of money for clothes.

spend money on: I spend a lot of money on clothes.

6. She came up to a creative solution.

come up with a solution. Come up to = walk towards someone.

7. It helps you look at your life from a different point of view.

Correct: from a different point of view.

8. Living abroad pushed me out of my comfort zone.

Correct: out of your comfort zone.
