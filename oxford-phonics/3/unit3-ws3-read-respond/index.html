📖 Read & Respond

Six short sentences tell a story about June and her huge mule. Read each one carefully — some questions are trickier than they look!

📗 Read the Story

Read each sentence, listen if you like, then answer the question below it.

1. June has a huge, gray mule.

The mule is huge.

2. The mule likes to eat a bone and a rose.

What does the mule eat?

3. June and the mule go home in June.

They go home in December.

4. At home, June gets a cute flute and a shiny cube.

What two things does June get at home?

5. The mule blows on the flute — what a huge joke!

The mule can play the flute.

6. June and the mule smile. Home is fun.

June and the mule are sad.
