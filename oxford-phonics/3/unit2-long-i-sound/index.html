SOUND FOCUS

Meet the Magic E! ✨

Today we practise the long i sound — the sound i makes when it says its own name, like in kite and time.

The rule: when a short word ends in i + consonant + e, the e stays quiet — but it works like magic and makes the i say "I"! 🪄

Listen to the difference:

kit → kite
pin → pine
rid → ride

You'll practise this sound in six mini-games: listening, sorting, spelling, rhyming, and reading. Ready? Let's go! 🚲
