# Submission ring — universal rule

Confirmed by Viktoria, 06.11.2026. Applies to every group and individual student, in teacher, student and parent views.

The outer submission ring is completed assigned tasks divided by all assigned tasks. A completed task counts once, regardless of its points or its share of the unit programme. All assigned tasks completed means 100%, even if only one task from the unit was assigned.

Assigned work with a future deadline belongs in the denominator. Unassigned work, N/A and drafts for future lessons do not. Work awaiting teacher confirmation is not completed. With no recorded assigned tasks, show an unknown percentage (–), never infer a percentage from a unit checklist.

Groups use assignment records in Mee. Individual plans use tasks assigned by the current lesson date; their due dates do not exclude assigned work. Unit checklists and weighted programme totals remain separate from the submission ring.

The start at Unit 7 for Movers mo1/mo2 is a scope exception, not a different denominator rule. Their Unit 7 checklist credit and actual Unit 8 marks do not create assignment records.
