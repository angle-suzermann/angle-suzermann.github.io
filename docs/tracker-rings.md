# Submission ring — universal rule

Confirmed by Viktoria, 06.11.2026. Applies to every group and individual student, in teacher, student and parent views.

The outer submission ring is completed assigned tasks divided by all assigned tasks. A completed task counts once, regardless of its points or its share of the unit programme. All assigned tasks completed means 100%, even if only one task from the unit was assigned.

Assigned work with a future deadline belongs in the denominator. Unassigned work, N/A and drafts for future lessons do not. Work awaiting teacher confirmation is not completed. With no recorded assigned tasks, show an unknown percentage (–), never infer a percentage from a unit checklist.

Group assignment records come from the teacher's table and online work. In Progress, `Assigned` means assigned and still pending, `Done` means submitted and awaiting confirmation, and `✓` means assigned and confirmed complete. Empty and `N/A` cells are excluded. Existing ticks are assignment evidence; a whole unit's empty programme is never the denominator. These states apply to unit sections, phonics and extra worksheets. Teachers cycle a circle through the states; no old empty cell is automatically converted into an assignment.

Online records are included alongside table records. An online record is merged with a table entry only when a unique exact task identity can be established (extra worksheet title or exact unit-and-section title); N/A and deleted/unassigned extra worksheets stay excluded. Do not guess that a numbered grammar exercise is the same task as an entire Grammar section. Individual plans use tasks assigned by the current lesson date; their due dates do not exclude assigned work. Weighted programme totals remain separate from the submission ring.

The start at Unit 7 for Movers mo1/mo2 is a scope exception, not a different denominator rule. Their confirmed Unit 7 and Unit 8 ticks count as assigned/completed work; empty Unit 8 cells remain unassigned. Earlier units are excluded, including phonics explicitly labelled with those units.
