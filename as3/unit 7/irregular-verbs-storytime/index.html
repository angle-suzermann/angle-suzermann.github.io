7Once Upon a Time

Every story needs to talk about the past — what a character did, saw, said or had. Today we meet ten verbs that don't just add -ed. They change completely. We call them irregular verbs.

Your Pupil's Book, Unit 7 — "Once upon a time" (p.82)

"Red Riding Hood wore a red coat. She said goodbye to her mum and dad and she went to her grandma's house."
"Aladdin stood in a cave and he saw a lamp."
🔊 2.17

Listen, point at the posters, and say the sentences with your teacher. Notice: wore, said, went, stood, saw — none of them end in -ed. That's our mission for today.
